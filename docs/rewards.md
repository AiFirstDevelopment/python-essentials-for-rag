# Trailhead Rewards

Trailhead Rewards is our free loyalty program for a fictional store, Trailhead Outfitters. You join when you create an account, and you start earning on your next order.

## Earning points

You earn one point for every dollar you spend, before tax and shipping. Points are added when your order ships, and taken back if you return the item. Members earn double points during the first week of every month and on their birthday month. Gift card purchases do not earn points, but spending a gift card does.

Members who spend $500 or more in a calendar year reach Summit level for the rest of that year and all of the next. Summit members earn one and a half points per dollar, get free express shipping on every order, and can return sale items for a refund to the original payment method instead of store credit.

## Using points

Every 100 points is worth $5 off a future order. You choose how many points to use at checkout, in steps of 100. Points cannot be exchanged for cash, cannot be moved to another account, and cannot be used to buy gift cards.

Points expire 18 months after the last time you earned or used any. Any purchase or redemption resets the clock for all your points. We email a reminder 30 days before points expire.

## Leaving the program

You can leave Trailhead Rewards at any time from your account settings. Leaving deletes your points, and they cannot be restored if you join again. Closing your account also ends your membership.
