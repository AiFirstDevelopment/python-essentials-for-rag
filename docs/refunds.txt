Returns and refunds (a sample policy for a fictional store, Trailhead Outfitters)

You can return any unused item within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be in their original
packaging with the tags attached. Sale items can be returned within 14 days and are refunded as store credit.

To start a return, sign in to your account, open the order and choose Return an item. We email a prepaid label.
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days of the item reaching our warehouse.

Gift cards, opened food and personalized items cannot be returned. Damaged or wrong items can be returned at any
time within 90 days at no cost; choose "It arrived damaged" when you start the return and attach a photo.
