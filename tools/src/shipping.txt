Shipping
This is a sample policy for a fictional store, Trailhead Outfitters.
Standard shipping is free on orders over $75 and takes 3 to 5 business days. Orders under $75 pay a flat $6.95. Express shipping costs $19.95 and arrives in 1 to 2 business days when the order is placed before 2 p.m. Eastern; orders placed later ship the next business day. Summit members of Trailhead Rewards get express shipping free on every order.
We ship to all 50 US states. Express is not available to P.O. boxes or to Alaska and Hawaii, which get standard shipping in 5 to 8 business days. International shipping is not available yet.
Large items, such as tents over 15 pounds and kayaks, ship by freight. Freight costs $49 for any order, takes 7 to 10 business days, and the carrier calls you to set a delivery time. Freight orders cannot be sent to a P.O. box or changed to express.

Tracking and delivery
Every order gets a tracking number by email as soon as it leaves the warehouse. Orders placed on a weekend leave on Monday. If an item is out of stock after you order, we ship the rest and email you a date for the missing item, and you pay no extra shipping for the second package.
If a package shows as delivered but you cannot find it, check with neighbors and around the building, then contact us within 7 days. We open a claim with the carrier and send a replacement or a refund once the claim is approved, usually within 5 business days. After 7 days we cannot open a claim.
To change the shipping address, contact us before the order ships; after it ships, the carrier may let you redirect it from the tracking page for a fee they charge. We cannot change the address of a freight order once it has been booked.
